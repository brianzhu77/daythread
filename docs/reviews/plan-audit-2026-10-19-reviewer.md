## Conclusion

The largest gap is **not just /page**. The accepted page model is absent as an end-to-end product: Material is still a separate entity, links do not become editable link Pages, pages have no parent/trash/URL properties, and there is no Linked from or page lifecycle. Existing domain and editor passes demonstrate useful slices, not this model. The editor also implements a smaller slash menu and phone toolbar than its documented item list. Explicit later simplifications must be distinguished from accidental omissions.

Status vocabulary: **missing** = no implementing model/command/UI at baseline; **partial** = part exists, required remainder absent; **evidence gap** = code/native behavior exists but cited evidence does not prove the promised experience; **superseded** = newer instruction changes the old presentation rule. “No consent located” means none in messages reviewed here; Product owns the broader original-message search. A Lead scheduling decision alone is not Brian's agreement to remove scope.
