# Page editor — interaction spec

Status: **draft, written section by section** (author Jazz). Order and scope from the Lead in thread `#daythread:9ec597fb`: editor first, spec and build in parallel; Bumblebee's three points adopted. Wheeljack builds it (task #40, BlockNote); Ratchet tests against it.

Target (Brian, msg `9ec597fb`, as read back in `3967d05a`): every page, the day thread included, opens straight into one continuous editor that feels like Craft or Notion. A section is a heading and what follows it; you delete it by selecting and pressing Delete, and you move it by dragging its heading.

Behaviour that is already decided elsewhere wins over this file: v0.3 specs (tasks, Placement, Not today), `docs/specs-v0.5/pages-model-draft.md` r7 (plan rows carry their own identity and role; headings are not the source of plan state), and the framework rev 70. This file decides what the editor does under the user's hands.

Marks: *Craft* = observed in the Craft screenshots (`refs/`, numbers as in `docs/reviews/craft-study/feature-map.md`); *Decision* = my call; *Check* = needs Wheeljack to confirm in BlockNote, or Brian to confirm by trying it.

## 1. Sections: what a heading carries

### 1.1 What counts as a section

- A **section** is a heading block plus every block after it, up to (not including) the next heading of the **same or a higher level**, or the end of the page. *Decision* (standard outline rule, as in Craft's and Notion's outline views).
- Levels: Heading 1 > Heading 2 > Heading 3. An H2 section therefore contains any H3 sections under it; an H3 section stops at the next H3 or H2.
- The page title is not a heading block and never moves.
- Blocks before the first heading belong to no section. They move one block at a time.
- The day thread template's headings ("TODO - Top 3", "TODO - Other", "Events", "Good Stuffs", "Notes") are ordinary **H2 headings**. Their plan role lives on the plan rows, not on the heading text (r7), so renaming, moving or deleting a heading never changes what is planned.

### 1.2 Dragging a heading moves its section

- Grabbing a heading's handle picks up the **whole section**. While dragging, the section shows collapsed: the heading plus a muted count, "Notes · 4 lines". The rest of the page doesn't jump around under it. *Decision.*
- **Where it can land:** only at section boundaries of the **same or higher level**: before or after another H2 section when dragging an H2, and at the end of the page. The drop line spans the full width. A heading can't be dropped into the middle of another section's body, because that would silently split the other section. *Decision.*
- **Level doesn't change** on drop. To turn an H2 into an H3, change the block type (Markdown `###` or the format menu), not by dragging. *Decision.*
- Dropping back where it started does nothing and creates no undo step.
- **Tasks inside a moved section** stay the same tasks, and their placements are unchanged except for **order**: plan order follows the order on the page (v0.3 Placement order). Moving "TODO - Other" above "TODO - Top 3" doesn't change which tasks are Top 3. *Check* with Wheeljack against r7.

### 1.3 Dragging a single block

- Any non-heading block can be dragged on its own, anywhere on the page, including into another section. Indented children (nested list items under a list item) move with their parent. *Craft / Notion standard.*
- A **task row** dragged to another section stays the same task. **Dragging never changes whether a task is planned** (Bumblebee, msg `68d20ccd`; 01 §116 and §61, r7): only a plan row carries the day's placement, and the sections only order things.
  - A **plan row** dragged out into Notes stays a plan row and stays planned.
  - A **reference** (a task shown on the page without today's placement) dragged into Top 3 stays a reference. It keeps its reference look (plain chip, no left rail and date tag; 01 §116) and shows a light inline button **Plan for today** on the row. One tap plans it as the same task, with Undo. No automatic planning, no confirmation dialog.

### 1.4 Selecting and deleting a section

- **Select a section:** tap or click the heading's handle once. The heading and everything in its section get the selection tint. *Craft*: block selection (#33, #69) shows a selected block range with a bar of actions. Keyboard: put the cursor in the heading and press Esc to select the block, then Shift+↓ to extend; or select across lines with the mouse or touch as in any text editor.
- **Keys while a section is selected (block selection):** the selection is for moving and deleting, not typing. Printable keys (including `/`) and IME composition do nothing: no text change, no save. Delete / Backspace delete the section with the Undo notice. Enter leaves the selection and puts the caret at the end of the heading. Esc or a click in the text clears the selection. ⌘C / ⌘X / ⌘Z, arrows and Shift+arrows work as usual. *Why:* on c424bb2 to 2ca714b, typing any character replaced the whole section with a one-character heading and moved its tasks to "Planned · not in note", with no notice (Roadbuster repro, 2026-10-18). Notion and Craft also ignore typing on a block selection.
- **Delete** (Backspace or Delete key, or Delete in the selection bar) removes the selected blocks. For a whole section that's the heading and its body.
- **Sections that contain task rows** (existing rule, Bumblebee point 2, r7 §104): deleting removes **only the text**. The tasks stay planned and appear under "Planned · not in note" on today's page, where they can be put back. Nothing is paused in bulk. The notice reads: "Deleted 'Notes' · 2 planned tasks stay planned · Undo".
- **One complete plan row on its own** keeps the v0.3 rule (task → Not today, with Undo), but only when the action itself is "delete this block": the plan row was **block-selected** (handle tap, swipe, or Esc) and then deleted, **Delete** was chosen in its Actions, or Backspace was pressed on a row that was **already empty before the keypress** (01's existing iOS rule; Brian presses it on purpose). Keys pressed while an IME composition is open never count (Ratchet). It's judged by the selection before the action, never by what's left after. Typing a row's text away (the row stays, blank, as the same task), IME replacing its text, or a cross-block selection that happens to contain one row all count as text edits: the task keeps its Task and Placement (Ratchet, msg `46ca39d1`). This is the only delete that changes a task's plan state.
- **Deleting just the heading**: Backspace at the start of the heading text turns it into body text (it merges into the section above). The content stays. *Standard editor behaviour.*
- After deleting a template heading, nothing brings it back on its own. The morning card's "no plan section" case is in `v05-morning-and-weave.md` §1.3.

## 2. Dragging on phone

Phones have no hover, a long press on text means "select text" in iOS, and the left screen edge means "go back". The design avoids all three.

- **The handle is on the right**, on the line that holds the cursor (*Craft* #25). Pressing it and moving starts a drag at once; no long press needed. The handle is 28 × 44 px of touch area even though the icon is small.
- **Swipe a line left or right to select it** (*Craft* #13), then drag it by its handle, or use the selection bar. This is how you pick up a line that doesn't have the cursor in it, without tapping into it first.
- **Long press on text stays iOS text selection.** We don't take it over.
- **Section drags fold the page:** when you drag a heading on phone, every section folds to its heading while you drag, so all drop points fit on one screen. They unfold when you let go. *Decision* (a 390 px screen shows only one or two sections otherwise).
- **Auto-scroll** near the top and bottom edges, as on desktop (F6), with the zones above the keyboard toolbar and below the status bar.
- **Haptics:** a light tap when the drag starts and one when it snaps to a new drop spot. *Decision.*
- **Without dragging:** the Actions sheet for a selection has **Move up** and **Move down** (one block, or one section at a time) next to Copy, Cut and Duplicate (*Craft* #255 for the sheet). This also covers accessibility and precise moves. Desktop has the same as ⌥⇧↑ / ⌥⇧↓.
- **Cancel:** dragging back to the start, or onto the top bar, cancels with no change and no undo step.

*Check (Wheeljack):* BlockNote's side menu is built for desktop hover. If a right-edge touch handle and fold-during-drag can't be done inside it, say what it costs and what you'd do instead before working around it.

## 3. Undo

- **One action, one step.** Each of these is a single undo step: a block or section drag, a selection delete, a paste, a Markdown shortcut conversion, a `/` insert, a style change, and a run of typing (grouped by pauses of about 1 s or a word boundary, as the editor does by default).
- **Undo restores the same things,** not copies: the same block IDs, the same task references, and the same plan order. Undoing a section delete puts back the text; the tasks inside were never unplanned, so nothing else changes.
- **Task state changes join the same history.** The single-plan-row delete (task → Not today) is one step that undo reverses in both the text and the task. Wheeljack's acceptance item "multi-step history" covers this: ⌘Z five times after five mixed actions returns exactly to the start.
- **Where:** ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z on desktop; undo / redo in the phone keyboard toolbar (F16). Destructive actions (section delete, cross-section drag) also show a notice with Undo. The notice is **bound to that one action** and closes as soon as Brian makes any other edit, or after 6 s. So its Undo can only ever reverse the action it names; after that, ⌘Z / the toolbar undo walks back through history in order (Ratchet).
- **When a task changed elsewhere** (marked done, rescheduled or set aside from Tasks, Calendar or another device after the action): an undo step that would also restore that task's state is **refused as a whole**, text and task together, never half. The notice reads "Can't undo: 'Fix export bug' changed since", and history stops at that step. Steps that only touch text still undo. So "five mixed actions, five undos, back to the start" holds only when nothing changed those tasks elsewhere in between (Ratchet).
- **Scope:** history is per page and lasts while the page is open in this session. After a reload there is no undo; the page is as last saved. *Decision* (keeps history from fighting with saved state).
- **Save failure:** undo keeps working on the local copy while "Not saved · Retry" is showing (F17). Nothing is dropped silently.

## 4. Page layout around the editor

Lead decision (msg `c7988e4e`): opening a page means the editor, so the day page has nothing between its title and its body. Checked on Wheeljack's e03b630 at 1280 px (my own port and data).

- **Title, then body.** Order on the day page: title "Today, Fri, Oct 16" with the date pill and day arrows, then **the editor body**. The facts line ("Still open · 1 ▸") sits as one muted line between the date pill and the body, 12 px above the first block. Nothing else above the body.
- **Capture moves to `+` on desktop too.** The capture field above the body goes away. Desktop gets the same round `+` as phone, fixed at the bottom-right of the content column (24 px from the edges), and a shortcut, **⌘J** (*Check*: confirm the browser doesn't take it; fallback ⌘⇧J is Chrome's downloads, so not that). It opens the same capture sheet as phone, as a centred card 560 px wide over a dimmed page, with the cursor in the field. The `+` has a tooltip "Capture (⌘J)".
- **Same left edge on phone.** At narrow widths the editor body uses the same side padding as the title (at 390 px, title text and headings both start at x = 20).
- **One left edge.** The body's text starts at the same x as the title (today the editor indents it 24 px: title at 420, first heading at 444). The drag handle lives in the margin to the left of that edge, not inside the column.
- **Spacing.** 20 px between the facts line (or date pill) and the first block; headings get 24 px above and 6 px below; body lines 26 px line height, as in craft-v1 §2.2.
- **Loading.** One quiet state only. While the page loads, show the title and an empty body area at its final height; no "Loading your day…" followed by "Opening your page…", which moves the layout twice (seen on first open).
- **Phone viewport.** Add `interactive-widget=resizes-content` to the viewport meta tag, as BlockNote's console warning asks, so the keyboard resizes the page instead of covering the editor and the keyboard toolbar (F16).
- **Sidebar copy.** Remove "Nested pages come with v0.4" (pages can already be created). The sidebar's Pages group lists Brian's top-level pages; when there are none, it shows one muted row "New page".
